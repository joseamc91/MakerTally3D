# MakerTally 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026.

## Developer

joseamc91

## App

MakerTally 3D is an offline Android calculator for estimating 3D printing costs and suggested selling prices.

## Accounts and online services

MakerTally does not require an account. It has no backend, developer-operated cloud, cloud synchronization, analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or remote crash reporting. It does not use Google Play Services and does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

## Data collection and sharing

MakerTally does not transmit personal data to the developer. The app does not collect data outside your device, sell data or share data with third parties. Filament identifiers distinguish local profiles; they are not used to identify or track you or your device.

## Local data

Filament profiles, calculator settings and preferences are stored in the app's private local storage. These include language, theme, electricity price, effective electricity tax percentage, heating power and time, machine cost, sale multiplier and filament sort order. Printing inputs and calculations are processed on the device.

MakerTally does not add its own encryption to these files. Access to private app storage is controlled by Android and the device configuration. If a local file is damaged, a local copy may be retained before recovery; it is not uploaded.

## Deletion, backup and device transfer

There is no server account or remote copy maintained by MakerTally to delete. You can clear the app's storage through Android settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its private local data through Android's system behavior.

MakerTally does not provide cloud backup or synchronization, and Android backup is disabled in its manifest. Certain system backup or device-transfer behaviors can depend on the Android version, device manufacturer and device configuration. MakerTally cannot guarantee the behavior of every manufacturer's transfer tools.

## Third-party components

MakerTally uses Android and Kotlin libraries for its interface, calculations, serialization and local storage. These components are not configured in MakerTally to send analytics or user data to a remote service. Third-party license notices are available at https://github.com/joseamc91/MakerTally3D/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

## External links and contact

The complete policy is included in the app and can be read offline. An optional online-policy button delegates a public web link to an external browser; MakerTally does not attach your profiles, settings or printing inputs. External browsers, GitHub and email providers operate under their own privacy policies when you choose to use them.

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer through GitHub Issues: https://github.com/joseamc91/MakerTally3D/issues.

Public developer email: joseamc91.dev@gmail.com.

Please do not post sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

## Policy updates

If the app's privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
