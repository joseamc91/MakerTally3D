# Google Play listing — en-US

## App name

MakerTally 3D

## Short description

Estimate 3D printing costs and a suggested selling price

## Full description

MakerTally 3D helps you estimate the cost of a 3D-printed part and a suggested selling price. Choose a filament profile, enter the part's weight and print time in hours and minutes, and see the result with a clear cost breakdown.

Costs based on your setup

• Material: purchase price and spool weight.
• Electricity: warm-up and printing consumption, price per kWh and an effective electricity tax percentage.
• Machine: an hourly allowance for wear, maintenance or depreciation.
• Suggested selling price: a multiplier applied to the piece cost, plus the additional machine cost.

Filament profiles

Add, edit or delete profiles, and activate the ones you want to use in the calculator. Check the price per kg and sort by name or price, low to high or high to low. Inactive profiles stay at the end of the list.

The starting profiles and settings are editable examples. Adjust prices, power estimates and rates to your purchases, printer and electricity bill. Profiles do not track stock.

Clear and adaptable

Exact decimal calculations keep intermediate costs unrounded. Expand Calculation details to check energy use, rates and gross margin. Built-in help explains electricity and taxes, heating, machine cost and the sale multiplier.

Use portrait mode or make use of the adaptive landscape layout, with side navigation and two-column views where appropriate. Choose Spanish or English and Light, Dark or System theme.

EUR and local-only use

MakerTally 3D 1.0 works exclusively in EUR (€). It works offline and keeps profiles and settings in the app's private local storage. No accounts, ads, analytics, telemetry or tracking. The complete privacy policy is available inside the app even without a connection.
